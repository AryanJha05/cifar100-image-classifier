PROJECT OVERVIEW

Academic AI/ML Problem Formulation

The CIFAR-100 dataset presents a notoriously challenging benchmark for deep vision models due to severe spatial limitations (32×32 pixels) combined with a high number of fine-grained categories (100 classes with only 500 training examples per class).

Spatial Constraint: 32×32 pixel resolution provides limited high-frequency texture details, demanding strong localized feature extraction.
Fine-grained Ambiguity: High intra-class variance and inter-class similarity (e.g. distinguishing between small mammals like otter vs. beaver, or seal vs. dolphin).
Inference Ready: Decoupled web client prepared for seamless integration with a Python FastAPI backend wrapping trained TensorFlow/Keras weights.

TRAINING METHODOLOGY

Data augmentation & normalization.

Techniques employed to maximize generalization and prevent catastrophic overfitting on small 500-sample subsets.

PREPROCESSING [0.0, 1.0]

Channel Normalization

Input pixels scaled from [0, 255] uint8 integers to [0.0, 1.0] float32 tensors, centering gradient propagation and stabilizing early convergence.

Formula: x_norm = x / 255.0
AUGMENTATION Spatial Invariance

Geometric Perturbations

Random horizontal flips (p=0.5), small degree rotations (±15°), and random horizontal/vertical translations (10%) to encourage orientation invariance.

Transforms: RandomFlip + RandomRotation
REGULARIZATION p = 0.3

Spatial Dropout

Stochastic neuron deactivation applied prior to dense classification layers, breaking co-adaptation and improving testing robustness.

Dropout(0.3) + BatchNormalization

LIMITATIONS

Known Constraints

  • Spatial downsampling loss: severely compressed real-world photos lose fine textures when downscaled to 32×32.
  • Superclass confusion: misclassifications predominantly occur among biologically adjacent fine classes within the same superclass.
FUTURE ROADMAP

Planned Improvements

  • Transfer learning: fine-tuning with pretrained MobileNetV2 or EfficientNet backbones.
  • Advanced data mix: CutMix and MixUp regularization to enhance boundary calibration.
